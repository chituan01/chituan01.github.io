Research

Bayesian methods for structured and spatially dependent data.

Learning structure instead of assuming it

Applied models usually fix the groups in advance — regions, sectors, cohorts — and estimate within them. Random partition models turn that around: the grouping itself is a parameter with a prior over partitions, so the posterior says both how many groups the data support and which units belong together. I am interested in partition priors that respect external structure, spatial contiguity above all, where the plausible partitions are the ones that carve a map into connected regions.

Computation

Partition models are awkward to sample. The state space is discrete and enormous, and naive Gibbs moves mix badly, which quietly limits how much model comparison anyone actually does. Part of my work is practical: writing samplers in C++ that are fast enough to make realistic simulation studies and model comparison possible.

Projects

Bayesian spatio-temporal clustering of Italian provinces Master's thesis, Politecnico di Milano, 2026

A regression model over I provinces and T periods in which provinces are randomly partitioned into clusters with cluster-specific coefficients, under a spatial product-partition prior with a boundary-length penalty, alongside Leroux-type CAR random effects. Estimation is a two-stage Gibbs procedure: sample partitions, select the loss-optimal partition, then re-estimate the remaining parameters conditional on it. Applied to female employment and early childhood education and care provision across Italian provinces, 2018–2022.

Online learning for budgeted multi-campaign bidding Course project, 2026

Algorithms for bidding across several advertising campaigns under a single shared budget and a conflict graph: UCB1 with and without the budget constraint, Combinatorial-UCB, a primal-dual best-of-both-worlds method, and sliding-window and change-detection variants for non-stationary environments.
