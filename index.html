I am a PhD student in Statistics and Computer Science at Bocconi University. My interest is in Bayesian methods for structured and spatially dependent data — models that let the data say where the boundaries are, rather than fixing the groups in advance — and in making the computation behind them fast enough to be useful.

Before Bocconi I completed my master's degree at Politecnico di Milano. My thesis developed a Bayesian spatio-temporal model in which Italian provinces are randomly partitioned into clusters with their own regression coefficients, combining a spatial product-partition prior with a boundary-length penalty and Leroux CAR effects. It is estimated by a Gibbs sampler written in C++ with RcppArmadillo and applied to early childhood education and care provision in Italy.

I work mainly in R and C++, with Python for machine learning, and I care about writing code that other people can actually run.

Interests — Bayesian nonparametrics · random partition models · spatio-temporal and areal data · MCMC and computation · applied statistics for social and education policy
