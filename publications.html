A Bayesian spatio-temporal product-partition model for areal data Master's thesis, Politecnico di Milano, 2026

A spatio-temporal regression model with a random partition of provinces into clusters carrying their own coefficients, under a spatial product-partition prior with a boundary-length cohesion penalty and Leroux CAR effects. Gibbs sampler in C++ (RcppArmadillo); applied to early childhood education and care provision and female employment in Italy, 2018–2022.
